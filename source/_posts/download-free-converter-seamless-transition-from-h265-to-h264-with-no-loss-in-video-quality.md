## Why Do We Need to Convert H.265 to H.264?

**For Streaming H265 4K Videos: Limited Bandwidth** 

The average bandwidth of America is only 10 Mbps reported in 2014\. But h265 files requires at least 15Mbps, which is widely unrealizable. The largest streaming company Netflix is dedicated at achieving [4K streaming](https://tools.techidaily.com/5kplayer/airplay/), but they can't reliably deliver 15Mbps video over one of the fastest connections around. Even if the TV sets were out there, even if the content was out there, can you deliver 15Mbps video to these UHD sets? The answer is unless you've got your own pipes, probably not. Convert h265 to h264 in this regard, is quite practical for most viewers since uploading or online streaming h265 videos are beyond years to come. 

**For playing h265 4K videos: Under Qualified Computer Specs;**

Even if you've got top-notch software like VLC for h265 decoding, unless you have got a robust hardware support to decode h265, you should let go the thought of playback h265 video smoothly without glitches. 

Compare H.265 to h264, we can see that H.265 is way more processor intensive, don't expect a simple firmware upgrade to get your gear to decode it. You'll need intel i7 core processor, a GPU above $200, a RAM at minimum of 8G-32G plus a SSD.
